About Marcelo

A coach who brings both professional expertise and personal understanding to every session.

His Story

From Recovery to Purpose

I've navigated the Remission Gap twice. I built my coaching practice to help you navigate it too.

My qualification for this work isn't a certificate in oncology — it's having been the patient twice, and having done the work myself to answer the same questions you're facing now: who am I outside of "patient," what do I do with this life, what comes next.

That lived experience sits alongside real coaching training: I hold a Diploma in Transformational Coaching from the Animas Centre for Coaching, and I'm an ICF-credentialed Associate Certified Coach (ACC). But the training gave me the structure. The two remissions gave me the understanding.

The Approach

Personal Philosophy

My coaching philosophy is rooted in the belief that adversity, while painful, carries within it the seeds of profound transformation. I do not believe in rushing the process. Real change takes time, courage, and the right support.

Drawing on positive psychology, strengths-based coaching, and mindfulness principles, I create a safe, non-judgemental space where you can explore your experience honestly and begin to build a new vision for your life.

My sessions are collaborative, not prescriptive. I do not pretend to have all the answers, but I know the right questions to ask. Together, we will discover what truly matters to you and chart a clear path forward.
