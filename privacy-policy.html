Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marcelo Marques Coaching ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data. We are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data Controller: Marcelo Marques, trading as Marcelo Marques Coaching, North Finchley, London, UK.
Contact: mmarques0365@gmail.com

1. What Data We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Name and email address via contact, booking, or sign-up forms
  • Any personal information shared in messages or during coaching sessions
  • Health-related information shared voluntarily during coaching (see Section 3 below)
  • Payment information processed securely by Stripe (we do not store card details)

Data collected automatically:

  • Website usage data via Google Analytics 4 (pages visited, time on site, general location)
  • Technical data including browser type, device type, and IP address
  • Cookies (see Section 7)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries and deliver coaching services
  • To send information you have requested, including our free guide and email newsletter
  • To process payments for services and digital products
  • To schedule and manage coaching sessions
  • To improve our website and understand visitor behaviour
  • To fulfil our legal and professional obligations

3. Special Category Data (Health Information)

Our coaching services are designed for cancer survivors and others navigating health transitions. This means you may share health-related information with us during the course of coaching. Under UK GDPR, health information is classified as "special category data" and is subject to additional protections.

We will only process health-related information:

  • With your explicit consent, given freely and in writing before coaching begins
  • For the sole purpose of providing coaching support to you
  • With strict confidentiality (see our Terms and Conditions)

You may withdraw this consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Important: Coaching is not a medical service. We do not provide medical advice or treatment. If you have concerns about your health, please consult your GP or a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide services you have requested
  • Explicit consent: Processing of health-related (special category) data
  • Consent: Sending marketing emails (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our website and services
  • Legal obligation: Where required by law

5. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers are based outside the UK. Where data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Kit (ConvertKit): Based in the USA. Complies with UK GDPR through standard contractual clauses.
  • Stripe: Based in the USA. Complies with UK GDPR through standard contractual clauses.
  • Google Analytics: Based in the USA. Data is anonymised before transfer. Google complies with UK GDPR.

6. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers:

  • Kit (ConvertKit): Email marketing and mailing list management
  • Stripe: Secure payment processing
  • Google Analytics: Anonymised website analytics
  • Web3Forms: Contact form processing
  • Calendly: Appointment scheduling

We may also disclose personal information if required by law or to protect the safety of you or others.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. Specifically:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4, to understand how visitors use our site. Data is anonymised and aggregated.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the website.

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Contact enquiries: 2 years
  • Client coaching records: 7 years (financial record-keeping obligations)
  • Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe
  • Website analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Payment records: 7 years (HMRC requirements)

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of your data in certain circumstances
  • Restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Data portability in certain circumstances
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any right, please email us at mmarques0365@gmail.com. We will respond within one calendar month.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

10. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. Payments are processed by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant. We do not store payment card details.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

11. Children

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. We will notify you of significant changes by email where we hold your contact details.
