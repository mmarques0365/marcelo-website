Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marcelo Marques Coaching ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website or use our services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data Controller: Marcelo Marques, trading as Marcelo Marques Coaching, North Finchley, London, UK. Email: mmarques0365@gmail.com

1. What Data We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name and email address when you complete a contact or booking form
  • Any information you share in messages or enquiries
  • Email address when you subscribe to our mailing list
  • Payment information when you purchase a service or digital product (processed securely by Stripe; we do not store card details)

Information collected automatically:

  • Website usage data via Google Analytics 4, including pages visited, time on site, and general location (country/region)
  • Technical data such as browser type, device type, and IP address

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide coaching services
  • To send you information you have requested, including our free guide and email newsletter
  • To process payments for services and digital products
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with our legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide services you have requested
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our website and services
  • Consent: Sending marketing emails (you can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing)
  • Legal obligation: Where required by law

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Kit (ConvertKit): Email marketing platform — stores your email address and manages mailing list subscriptions
  • Stripe: Payment processing — handles payment transactions securely
  • Google Analytics: Website analytics — processes anonymised usage data
  • Web3Forms: Contact form processing — transmits your enquiry to us
  • Calendly: Booking system — manages appointment scheduling

All third-party providers are required to protect your data and may only use it to provide services to us.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Contact enquiries: up to 2 years
  • Client records: 7 years (to comply with financial record-keeping requirements)
  • Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe
  • Website analytics: 26 months (Google Analytics default)

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience and analyse website traffic. Specifically:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4, used to understand how visitors use our site. Data is anonymised.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the website.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at mmarques0365@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant. Our website uses HTTPS encryption.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
