Understanding the Remission Gap

Why Remission Coaching?

When cancer treatment ends, most people expect to feel relief. Instead, many feel lost, frightened, and unsure of who they are. This is the Remission Gap.

Coaching provides a confidential, structured space to process your experience, rebuild your identity, and move forward with a clear vision. Unlike therapy, coaching is forward-looking and action-oriented. It is about building what comes next.

Read the FAQ
  • Clarity & Direction Cut through the noise. Identify what you truly want and map a clear path to get there.
  • Strength-Based Approach Build on your existing strengths rather than focusing on what's broken.
  • Accountability & Support A dedicated partner who holds the space and keeps you moving forward between sessions.
  • Sustainable Change Coaching creates shifts that stick "” in mindset, habits, and how you relate to yourself.
